1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PLATFORM PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

SYNTHESIS is a research-grade mobile astrophysics observatory and exoplanetary
discovery pipeline. Designed for the era of next-generation space missions (NASA
James Webb Space Telescope, ESA PLATO, and the Nancy Grace Roman Space
Telescope), SYNTHESIS bridges the gap between deep theoretical astrophysics and
practical, hands-on observational astronomy.

The platform transforms a consumer Android device into a complete mission control
center capable of analyzing multi-mission astronomical catalogs (Kepler, K2, TESS),
simulating cosmic spacetime metrics, and directly commanding robotic smart telescopes
(ZWO Seestar, DwarfLab DWARF, Vaonis, Unistellar, and ASCOM Alpaca systems) to
participate in global planetary transit observation campaigns.

Every calculation in SYNTHESIS uses established astrophysical physics formulations,
incorporating General Relativity, Keplerian orbital mechanics, radiative transfer,
magnetohydrodynamics, and stellar asteroseismology.

2. CORE SCIENTIFIC CAPABILITIES
================================================================================

2.1. Exoplanet Candidate Detection & Transit Analysis
-----------------------------------------------------
- Multi-Mission Photometric Ingestion:
  Direct integration with NASA Exoplanet Archive, Kepler Space Telescope, and
  Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) full-frame and target-pixel data.
- Box-Least-Squares (BLS) & Transit-Least-Squares (TLS):
  Identifies periodic transit dimming signals within stellar light curves.
  Calculates Signal-to-Pink-Noise (SPN) and false alarm probability.
- Precise Orbital Mechanics Extraction:
  Derives orbital period (P in days), transit epoch (BJD_TDB), transit duration
  (T14 in hours), impact parameter (b), and semi-major axis (a in Astronomical Units).
- Physical Planet Characterization:
  Computes planetary radius in Earth radii (R_Earth) and Jupiter radii (R_Jup) by
  combining transit depth (delta = (R_p / R_star)^2) with host star effective
  temperature (T_eff), surface gravity (log g), and stellar radius (R_Sun).

2.2. Habitability Assessment & Equilibrium Thermodynamics
---------------------------------------------------------
- Planetary Equilibrium Temperature:
  Calculates T_eq assuming Bond albedo and uniform heat redistribution:
    T_eq = T_star * sqrt(R_star / (2 * a)) * (1 - A_B)^(1/4)
- Habitable Zone Boundary Modeling (Kopparapu et al.):
  Computes the Conservative and Optimistic Habitable Zone limits (Recent Venus,
  Runaway Greenhouse, Maximum Greenhouse, and Early Mars thresholds).
- Earth Similarity Index (ESI):
  Computes multi-parameter ESI based on radius, density, escape velocity, and
  surface temperature to quantify analog proximity to Earth.

2.3. Spectral Analysis & Biosignature / Technosignature Detection
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Biosignature Matrix Assessment:
  Evaluates chemical disequilibrium in planetary atmospheres by measuring
  coexistence of methane (CH4), molecular oxygen (O2), ozone (O3), water vapor (H2O),
  carbon dioxide (CO2), and dimethyl sulfide (DMS).
- Non-Equilibrium Chemical Confidence:
  Assigns a rigorous statistical score (0 - 100%) indicating whether atmospheric
  compounds can be explained solely by volcanic/geological outgassing or require
  biological metabolic processes.
- Technosignature Detection Engine:
  Scans for artificial artifacts in stellar light curves:
  * Non-Keplerian asymmetric transit ingress/egress (artificial megastructures)
  * Narrowband continuous and pulsed laser emissions
  * Megastructure transits (Dyson swarms, stellar engines) with anomalous chromatic
    extinction ratios across optical and infrared passbands.

2.4. Dilution Deblending & Harmonic Stitching
--------------------------------------------
- Neighboring Star Flux Dilution:
  Corrects shallow transit signals blended by nearby stars inside the photometric
  aperture. Calculates true planet radius using arcsecond neighbor separation and
  delta-magnitude corrections:
    delta_true = delta_observed * (1 + 10^(-0.4 * delta_mag))
- Harmonic Period Stitching:
  Identifies and resolves orbital resonance harmonics (P/2, 2P, 3P/2) that often
  cause algorithmic misclassification of multi-planet systems.

3.1. ESA PLATO Asteroseismology & Stellar Age Determination
----------------------------------------------------------
- Acoustic p-mode and gravity g-mode pulsation modeling for bright dwarf and subgiant stars.
- Calculates Large Frequency Separation (Delta_nu) and Frequency of Maximum Power (nu_max).
- Seismic Scaling Relations:
    M / M_Sun = (nu_max / nu_max,Sun)^3 * (Delta_nu / Delta_nu,Sun)^(-4) * (T_eff / T_eff,Sun)^(3/2)
    R / R_Sun = (nu_max / nu_max,Sun) * (Delta_nu / Delta_nu,Sun)^(-2) * (T_eff / T_eff,Sun)^(1/2)
- Delivers stellar age estimates with +/- 10% precision, unlocking the evolutionary
  timeline of exoplanetary architectures.

3.2. NASA JWST Atmospheric Transmission Spectroscopy
----------------------------------------------------
- High-fidelity synthetic transmission spectra covering 0.6 to 12.0 micrometers
  (NIRISS, NIRSpec, and MIRI instrument simulation).
- Molecular absorption cross-sections:
  * H2O bands at 1.4, 1.9, and 2.7 um
  * CO2 fundamental vibration band at 4.3 um
  * CH4 absorption at 3.3 and 7.7 um
  * NH3 detection in cool giant atmospheres
  * Dimethyl Sulfide (DMS) biomarker probe at 3.4 um
- Rayleigh scattering slope calculation to determine mean molecular weight and
  cloud-deck pressure levels (gray aerosol extinction).

3.3. Roman Space Telescope Gravitational Microlensing (Rogue Planets)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Einstein Ring Radius & Crossing Timescale:
  Models gravitational deflection of background galactic bulge stars by foreground
  unbound rogue planets.
    theta_E = sqrt((4 * G * M_lens / c^2) * (D_s - D_l) / (D_l * D_s))
- Finite Source Effect & Caustic Crossings:
  Simulates planetary magnification spikes (Paczyński curve) with finite-source
  limb darkening, resolving planets down to Earth mass in wide or interstellar orbits.

3.4. FLRW Big Bang Early Universe Cosmology & Dark Energy Simulator
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Solves the Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker (FLRW) dynamical equations:
    H^2(z) = H_0^2 * [Omega_r * (1+z)^4 + Omega_m * (1+z)^3 + Omega_k * (1+z)^2 + Omega_Lambda * f(z)]
- Dynamic Dark Energy Equation of State:
  Chevallier-Polarski-Linder (CPL) parametrization: w(a) = w_0 + w_a * (1 - a).
- Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) Acoustic Peaks:
  Calculates sound horizon at recombination (r_s ~ 147 Mpc) and multipole angular
  scale (l_1 ~ 220).
- Big Bang Nucleosynthesis (BBN):
  Computes primordial mass fractions of 4He (Helium-4), Deuterium/Hydrogen (D/H),
  and 7Li as a function of the baryon-to-photon ratio eta.

3.5. Kerr Spinning Black Hole General Relativity & Gravitational Waves
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Metric of Stationary Axisymmetric Spacetime:
  Computes horizon radii r_+ (outer event horizon) and r_- (Cauchy horizon) for
  dimensionless spin parameter a* = J / (G * M^2 / c) in the range [-1.0, 1.0].
- Ergosphere & Penrose Process:
  Calculates the boundary of static limit where spacetime frame-dragging forces
  matter and light to co-rotate with the singularity.
- Innermost Stable Circular Orbit (ISCO):
  Determines accretion disk inner edge (r_ISCO = 6 R_g for Schwarzschild, down to
  1 R_g for an extreme prograde Kerr black hole).
- Gravitational Wave Chirp Simulation:
  Simulates quadrupole binary black hole coalescence (LIGO/Virgo/LISA wave profiles),
  computing chirp mass M_chirp = (m1 * m2)^(3/5) / (m1 + m2)^(1/5), instantaneous
  frequency evolution f_GW(t), and peak strain amplitude h_c.

3.6. Exomoon Tidal Dissipation & Subsurface Ocean Thermodynamics
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Models viscoelastic tidal heating in moons orbiting gas giants (Jupiter/Saturn analogs).
- Computes tidal dissipation energy flux E_tidal from orbital eccentricity e,
  semi-major axis, and Love number k_2 / Q.
- Computes equilibrium ice-shell thickness and evaluates hydrothermal vent energy
  availability to support chemosynthetic sub-surface ocean biospheres.

3.7. Planetary Magnetosphere Shielding & Stellar Wind Dynamics
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Calculates stellar wind dynamic ram pressure:
    P_dyn = rho_sw * v_sw^2
- Chapman-Ferraro Magnetopause Stand-Off Distance:
    R_mp = [ (2 * mu_0 * B_0^2 * R_p^6) / (P_dyn) ]^(1/6)
- Evaluates atmospheric retention versus stellar coronal mass ejections (CME),
  determining vulnerability to solar/stellar wind stripping over billion-year epochs.

3.8. 1D Radiative-Convective Equilibrium Planetary Climate Engine
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Interactive climate simulator accepting stellar insolation (S / S_0), surface
  pressure (P_0 in bar), and greenhouse gases (CO2, H2O, CH4).
- Computes runaway greenhouse transition thresholds, water loss runaway, and
  surface equilibrium temperatures.

2. Smart Telescope Hotspot Gateways:
   Directly probes known default telescope hotspot AP gateways:
   * 192.168.110.1:4700 (ZWO Seestar default hotspot)
   * 192.168.88.1:9988   (DWARF II / 3 default hotspot)

8. GLOSSARY OF PHYSICAL PARAMETERS & METRICS
================================================================================

- R_p / R_star: Ratio of planetary radius to host stellar radius.
- Depth (ppm): Transit depth in parts per million (10,000 ppm = 1% dimming).
- T14 (hours): Total transit duration from first contact (ingress start) to fourth contact (egress finish).
- BJD_TDB: Barycentric Julian Date in Barycentric Dynamical Time, standard for high-precision exoplanet timing.
- Delta_nu (uHz): Asteroseismic large frequency separation, proportional to the square root of mean stellar density.
- nu_max (uHz): Frequency of maximum acoustic pulsation power, proportional to surface gravity and effective temperature.
- a* (Kerr spin): Dimensionless black hole spin parameter (-1 <= a* <= 1).
- r_ISCO: Radius of the innermost stable circular orbit of a black hole accretion disk.
- M_chirp: Gravitational wave chirp mass governing the orbital frequency evolution of coalescing compact binaries.
- w_0, w_a: Parametrization of the dark energy equation of state w(a) = P / rho.
